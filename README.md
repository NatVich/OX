# OX

A kid-friendly OX (tic-tac-toe) game, hand-drawn on graph paper. Play against a friend on the same screen, or against the computer.

Play online: https://natvich.github.io/OX/

- Three computer levels: Easy, Medium and Hard. Hard never loses, so a draw counts as a win.
- Each opponent keeps its own score, saved on your device.
- After a win, tap the board to play again. After a draw, a new round starts by itself. The loser goes first.
- Pen-and-paper animations: the grid draws itself, marks stamp down, the winning three cheer, and the board is rubbed out between rounds.
- Sounds with an on/off button, and confetti when you win.
- Works in light and dark mode, on desktop and phone. Animations turn off if your device is set to reduce motion.

## Play

Open `index.html` in any web browser. No install or build step.
